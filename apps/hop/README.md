# Hop

A one-button game. It's in Games on your unidex, after Flappy, Dino, Stack and Jetpack.

Blocks come from the right. **B** hops over them: a tap clears a low block, and **holding B** on the way up hops
higher, over a tall one. Each block you clear is a point, and every 5 points it gets a little faster. Touch a block
and the round is over.

Nothing moves until your first press. **Hold A** leaves a round.

The Games app keeps your best score. No permissions. Needs firmware newer than v1.9.
