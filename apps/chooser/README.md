# Chooser

Can't decide? Number your options, pick that many squares (2 to 9) with **A**, and press **B**: the highlight hops
along the squares, slows down, and stops on one at random.

**Hold B** shows the tally: how many times each square number has won, over every spin. Hold B there to clear it
(it asks first).

**What it keeps:** the number of squares you last used and the tally, in the app's own folder on your SD card.

**Coming from the built-in Chooser?** Chooser used to come with the device. When the device updated to the firmware
without it, your tally was moved into this app's folder, so it's all here when you install it.
