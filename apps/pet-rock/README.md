# Pet Rock

A rock that lives on your unidex. It doesn't need feeding.

- **B** pats it. Its mood drifts from happy to content to bored to fast asleep the longer it goes without one.
- **A** listens: it has a few things to say.
- **Hold B** gives it a new coat of paint: stripes, dots, zigzag, stars or speckles.

The header counts the days you've had it. Everything is kept on the card. No permissions.
