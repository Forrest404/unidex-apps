# Focus

A Pomodoro timer: focus for a while, then take a short break.

- **B** starts, pauses and resumes. When the focus time is up, **B** starts the break.
- **A** (before you start) picks the lengths: 25 + 5, 50 + 10 or 15 + 3 minutes.
- **Hold B** stops.

The minutes left come from the clock, so the timer is right whenever you look, even after your unidex has gone
to sleep. It can't make a sound or wake you when time's up, so glance at it. The header counts today's sessions.
It needs the clock to be set. No permissions.
