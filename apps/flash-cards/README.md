# Flash Cards

Three decks: European capitals, times tables (2 to 12) and chemical symbols.

- On the first screen, **A** picks a deck and **B** starts 20 shuffled cards.
- **B** flips a card. Then **B** if you knew it, or **A** if you didn't: a missed card comes back a few cards later.
- At the end you see how many you got right first time. Your best score for each deck is kept.
- **Hold A** goes back to the decks.

No permissions.
