# Quote of the Day

**B** fetches today's quote over your unidex's WiFi and keeps it for the rest of the day. Until you do, and
whenever there's no connection, it shows a proverb of the day from its own list of thirty.

The text is sized to fit: short quotes are big, long ones smaller.

**What it connects to:** `zenquotes.io` (`/api/today`), over HTTPS, once when you press B. Nothing about you is
sent. Quotes are provided by [ZenQuotes](https://zenquotes.io), credited on screen as their free API asks.
