# Moon

A badge plugin. Open **Badge** and it's there after your own badges: the moon as it looks tonight, worked out from
the date (the dark part is filled in), its phase, how much of it is lit and how many days to the next full moon.

It's calculated on the device from the average length of the moon's cycle, so it can be up to about a day out. It
needs the clock to be set. No permissions.
